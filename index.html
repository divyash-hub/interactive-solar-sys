    About HELIOS

    A real-time, interactive model of the Solar System, designed and developed by Divyash Agrawal.

    How it works

    Planet positions are calculated from NASA/JPL Keplerian orbital elements (J2000), solving Kepler's equation every frame — so the layout you see matches the real sky for any date. Distances are compressed on a logarithmic scale so everything fits on screen, while the shape and tilt of each orbit stay true. Sizes are enlarged for visibility.

    Rendering

    Built with Three.js and WebGL: physically based materials, real-time shadows (eclipses and Saturn's ring shadows), a day/night shader with city lights for Earth, atmospheric scattering rims, HDR bloom and filmic tone mapping.
